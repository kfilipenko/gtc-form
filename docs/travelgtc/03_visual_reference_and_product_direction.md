## 3. Interpreted Product Idea

TravelGTC is not just a travel brochure site.

The Project Owner approved the final concept name:

```text
Travel Network Lab
```

The references describe a travel network platform with four connected promises:

1. travel as lifestyle and personal freedom;
2. club/community participation;
3. creation of routes, tours and events by users or organizers;
4. partner/network business model around recommendations, community growth and travel services.

The website should make this idea clear quickly and lead the visitor to one of several actions:

1. create a travel idea;
2. learn about opportunities;
3. join or contact the club;
4. discuss partnership;
5. request a presentation or consultation.

## 5. First Landing Page Blocks

1. Hero: emotional headline, travel/community image, primary CTA.
2. Benefit strip: travel worldwide, community, own tours/events, business growth.
3. How it works: choose idea, gather people, use platform, travel/create event, grow network.
4. What you get: best offers, create tours/events, international community, mobile tools.
5. Create your travel idea: simple tags or form-like selection.
6. Business model: no office, low costs, recommendations, unlimited potential, education/support.
7. Trust/emotional banner: travel changes life, network gives freedom.
8. Final CTA: contact, WhatsApp/MAX, request presentation.
9. Footer: navigation, contacts, social links and legal links.

## 6. Visual Direction

1. Deep navy and ocean-blue foundation.
2. Bright cyan accents for technology, routes and icons.
3. Bright green buttons for primary conversion actions.
4. White or very light content bands for readability.
5. Large travel photography, preferably with people and community scenes.
6. Rounded cards and icon-driven benefit blocks.
7. Strong mobile-preview block to signal future platform/app capability.

## 7. Action-First Interpretation

The first website should not be only a presentation.

It should include at least one practical interaction:

1. create travel idea;
2. request consultation;
3. request presentation;
4. discuss participation in messenger;
5. leave contact details for club/partner follow-up.

## 8. Risks To Clarify

1. Partner income and reward language must be legally and commercially approved.
2. Travel services, booking, agency duties and liability boundaries must be defined before public claims.
3. If a club model is used, membership terms may be required.
4. If leads are collected, privacy policy and consent text are required.
5. If WhatsApp/MAX communication is used, contact data handling must be documented.
