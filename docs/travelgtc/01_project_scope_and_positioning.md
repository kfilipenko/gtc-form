## 1. Purpose

This is the first working document for the TravelGTC website project.

Its purpose is to define what the site must become before design, page structure, content and implementation decisions are fixed.

## 2. Product Goal

TravelGTC is implemented under the final concept name `Travel Network Lab`.

Travel Network Lab is a travel network and club platform that helps people create travel ideas, gather a group, join trips/events, use partner opportunities and grow a community-based travel business around recommendations, shared experiences and support.

## 3. Initial Positioning

Approved direction after Project Owner task definition:

```text
Travel Network Lab should be positioned as a modern travel club with business opportunity:
travel as lifestyle, community, own routes/events, partner offers and network development.
The first website must first show the dream of travel, then the power of community,
then calmly introduce the business model.
```

## 4. Participants / Users

| Participant | Role | Primary action |
|---|---|---|
| Website visitor | Potential traveler, club member or partner | Understand the offer and choose the next action. |
| Traveler / club member | Person interested in trips, events and community | Join the club, create a travel idea or request consultation. |
| Travel idea author | Person who wants to gather people around a route, tour or event | Describe the idea, audience and destination. |
| Partner / network participant | Person or company interested in referrals, community growth or travel products | Learn the business model and discuss participation. |
| Project Owner | Product and business owner | Define goals, provide source materials and approve public positioning. |
| Website administrator | Future operator | Publish content, review inquiries and maintain public pages. |

## 6. Questions To Resolve Before Design

1. What is the exact commercial model: club, referral network, agency model, subscription, partner program or a combination?
2. What is the primary first audience: travelers, community leaders, partners, travel organizers or all of them?
3. Is the first release a public website, an action-first application or a hybrid?
4. What is the first conversion action: create a travel idea, contact in WhatsApp/MAX, join the club or request presentation?
5. What documents, rules or conditions must be published before collecting applications?
6. What languages must be supported from the first release?
7. What external services, forms, payments, booking tools or CRM integrations are expected?
8. Which claims about discounts, income, rewards, partner benefits and support require legal/commercial approval?

## 7. First Implementation Stage

Stage 1 now focuses on:

1. approved product goal and positioning;
2. first static public prototype in `projects/travelgtc/public/`;
3. first content map and navigation based on the club/network model;
4. selected visual assets optimized from the image inbox;
5. simple frontend form for travel ideas and contact actions;
6. future backend/legal/deploy work before real lead collection or publication.

## 8. What The Project Is Not Yet

Until the Project Owner confirms the scope, the project is not yet:

1. a finalized travel agency website;
2. a booking platform;
3. a payment service;
4. a public legal offer;
5. a confirmed investment, income or guaranteed reward proposal;
6. a ready-to-publish commercial website.

## 10. Visual Direction

1. Full-width travel hero with real or generated travel imagery.
2. Deep navy base, cyan travel-tech accents and bright green action buttons.
3. Strong emotional headline about travel, community and network growth.
4. Cards for benefits, club services, business opportunity and mobile tools.
5. Simple process block: choose idea, gather people, use platform, travel/create event, grow network.
6. Contact actions through WhatsApp/MAX or similar direct channels.
7. Mobile-first presentation is important from the first implementation stage.
