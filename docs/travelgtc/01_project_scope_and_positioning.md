## 1. Purpose

This is the first working document for the TravelGTC website project.

Its purpose is to define what the site must become before design, page structure, content and implementation decisions are fixed.

## 2. Product Goal

Project Owner to fill:

```text
Describe the practical goal of travelgtc.com in one paragraph.
```

## 3. Initial Positioning

Current working assumption:

```text
TravelGTC is a new GTC web project for a travel-related service or product.
The exact audience, commercial model, first pages, legal conditions and visual style
must be confirmed by the Project Owner before implementation.
```

## 4. Participants / Users

| Participant | Role | Primary action |
|---|---|---|
| Website visitor | Potential client or partner | Understand the offer and choose the next action. |
| Project Owner | Product and business owner | Define goals, provide source materials and approve public positioning. |
| Website administrator | Future operator | Publish content, review inquiries and maintain public pages. |

## 5. Materials To Provide

Place source images here:

```text
projects/travelgtc/public/assets/images/inbox/
```

Recommended first materials:

1. logo or temporary brand mark;
2. photographs or illustrations connected with the future offer;
3. examples of sites or visual references the Project Owner likes;
4. text draft describing the service;
5. list of required languages;
6. list of first pages to create.

## 6. Questions To Resolve Before Design

1. What is the exact business purpose of TravelGTC?
2. Who is the primary audience?
3. Is the first release a public website, an action-first application or a hybrid?
4. What action should the visitor take first?
5. What documents, rules or conditions must be published?
6. What languages must be supported from the first release?
7. What external services, forms, payments, booking tools or CRM integrations are expected?

## 7. First Implementation Stage

Stage 1 should focus on:

1. approved product goal and positioning;
2. domain/publication plan for `travelgtc.com`;
3. first content map and navigation;
4. selected visual assets from the image inbox;
5. first public page or prototype after Project Owner approval.

## 8. What The Project Is Not Yet

Until the Project Owner confirms the scope, the project is not yet:

1. a finalized travel agency website;
2. a booking platform;
3. a payment service;
4. a public legal offer;
5. a ready-to-publish commercial website.
