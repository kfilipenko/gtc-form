## 1. Summary

The home section `Как вы становитесь участником или изучаете роль Ambassador` was simplified from six process cards to three clear columns.

The heading remains unchanged.

## 2. Published Steps

1. `Диалог с Мирой`
   - The visitor chooses the first question or writes their own.
   - Mira helps clarify Travel Advantage, Free Guest Pass, VIP Membership, Membership levels, trips, events and Lifestyle Ambassador role.

2. `Официальная ссылка`
   - When the visitor is ready to continue, Mira gives the correct official link for registration, guest access or Membership review on MWR Life / Travel Advantage pages.

3. `Сопровождение TravelGTC`
   - If the visitor needs help with choice, country-specific conditions or a concrete situation, they can continue messaging or receive a response from a TravelGTC staff member.

## 3. Communication Rule

The public page should not emphasize a human-vs-AI distinction.

Use natural service wording:

- Mira;
- dialogue;
- messaging;
- TravelGTC support;
- TravelGTC staff member where needed.

The legal pages may still disclose AI/automated processing because privacy and terms must explain how chat history and CRM data are handled.
