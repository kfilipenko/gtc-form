Platform overview

CrewPortGlobal portal overview and operating standards

CrewPortGlobal is an international maritime workflow platform for seafarers, shipowners, vessel operators, ship managers, agents and platform review teams.

Public overview v1.0 Published 2026-07-08 Stage 1 technology and workflow service
Action-first maritime workflow Participants create records, upload evidence, receive tasks, review terms, prepare contracts and move controlled work objects through a traceable process.

01

What CrewPortGlobal is built to do

CrewPortGlobal organizes maritime crewing work around structured data, reviewed evidence, controlled tasks and clear participant authority. The portal is not intended to be a reading library; it is designed to support practical work and preserve an audit trail before the next stage of a workflow is allowed.

Current Stage 1 position CrewPortGlobal is operated as a technology and workflow service. It does not present itself as a licensed manning agency or employment agency unless and until required approvals are confirmed. Recruitment or placement activity requiring licensing must be performed only after regulatory confirmation or through appropriately licensed partners.

02

Participants and roles

SeafarerCreates and maintains a professional maritime profile, uploads documents, reviews opportunities and must not be charged recruitment or placement fees for access to work opportunities.
Shipowner / employerRegisters company, vessel and crew-request context, reviews controlled candidate presentations and may initiate contracts and agent appointment processes.
Agent / agent companyActs within verified authority, prepares work objects, assists with contract drafting and supports both sides within controlled platform boundaries.
Platform review teamReviews profiles, documents, companies, vessels, matching results, shortlists and presentation readiness.
Platform Administration / ControlReviews authority, access, conflicts, assignment exceptions, governance blockers and admin-level permission changes.
Billing / commercial groupHandles future Service Orders, commercial addenda, price-basis records and billing evidence.

Implemented functional areas

  • Shared application shell, navigation and multilingual public pages.
  • User registration, authentication, email-code access and personal cabinet entry.
  • Seafarer profile workspace with structured forms, autosave, completeness navigation and document readiness controls.
  • Employer and shipowner intake for company, vessel and crew-request context.
  • Operator and team review surfaces for profiles, companies, vessels, documents, vacancies, matching and shortlists.
  • Vacancy and job-search flows with controlled review before candidate-facing or employer-facing use.
  • Agent organization scope, authority evidence, object assignments, agent tasks and managed object workspaces.
  • Shipowner-to-agent framework offer, agent acceptance, platform authority evidence, one-active-manager assignment and participant notification ledger entries.
  • Unified Contract Agreement Workspace foundation and agent-assisted drafting of direct seafarer-shipowner contracts.
  • Public legal documents hub, access administration and owner/admin executable menu links.

04

How work moves through the portal

The portal computes participant tasks from business records. A task is visible only when a participant, representative or team has a valid relationship to the current object and process stage.

information stream -> working object -> object state -> business-process stage -> responsible participant or group -> visible task -> exact working object link -> evidence and audit record -> next computed stage
Evidence and audit principle Every important action should leave a record: registration, email confirmation, profile save, document upload, review result, candidate presentation, agreement offer, authority record, assignment, notification, draft update and contract review request.

05

International maritime standards alignment

MLC 2006 alignment CrewPortGlobal is designed to support no-fee access for seafarers, seafarer review of contract conditions, wage and repatriation visibility, complaint routes, controlled recruitment and placement evidence, and human review before candidate presentation or contract-critical steps.
STCW alignment CrewPortGlobal treats rank, certificates, training, endorsements, watchkeeping-related qualifications and document readiness as structured evidence rather than free marketing text.
What the portal does not certify CrewPortGlobal does not automatically certify that a participant, vessel, document, contract or employment decision satisfies all applicable law. The portal provides workflow discipline, evidence structure, review controls and audit records that support lawful decision-making by responsible parties and competent professionals.

06

Contracts, authority and agents

CrewPortGlobal separates legal authority, operational workflow and commercial terms. A physical person should control personal account credentials. A representative may receive operational rights only through verified authority evidence and platform assignment. A shipowner sends an offer to an agent; the appointment becomes active only after the agent accepts the standard framework agreement inside the platform and authority records are created.

Agent-assisted drafting Agent-assisted drafting is preparation, not automatic final signature by the agent. Direct seafarer-shipowner contract terms must remain visible to the real parties for review and approval.
No-fee boundary CrewPortGlobal must not be used to charge seafarers recruitment, placement or employment-access fees. Optional services for seafarers must be voluntary, separated from job access and not a condition for matching.

CrewPortGlobal is not

  • a source of fake vacancies, fake employers or decorative marketplace activity;
  • a system that charges seafarers for recruitment or placement;
  • an automatic legal certification system or legal-advice substitute;
  • an automatic employment-decision engine;
  • a tool that turns an external private contract into platform authority without CrewPortGlobal controlled authority and assignment process.
